# Page content

The page answers three questions in order, one section each. The content comes from
`content/facts.md`; this doc says how to arrange it. See [acceptance checks](acceptance-checks.md)
for what is enforced.

## #about: who he is

Two or three sentences. Lead with the one-line summary from the facts file, then
the nine-in-ten commit share, then the Dubai background. No list here.

## #shipped: what he has shipped

One short block per project, in the order the facts file lists them. Each block is a
heading plus two or three sentences, with the strongest number in the first
sentence. Keep the incident in "Making repositories safe for coding agents"; an
honest failure is more convincing than another success.

Open source and writing go at the end of this section as plain links.

## #contact: how to reach him

Email first, then GitHub, LinkedIn and the website. Real `mailto:` and `https:`
links. No contact form.

## Tone

Write like a careful engineer explaining his work to another one. If a sentence would
fit on any portfolio, cut it.
